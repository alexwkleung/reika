# Contributors

[← README](../README.md)

Reika is built and maintained by one developer, and several people have made it better. A huge thank you to
everyone here and anyone who uses Reika in their local AI workflow.

## Code and documentation

- [@xiaoyu607](https://github.com/xiaoyu607): Simplified Chinese translations of the pre-install
  pages and the localized documentation site ([#700](https://github.com/alexwkleung/reika/pull/700)).
- [@zlc-131](https://github.com/zlc-131): Simplified Chinese translation of the support page
  ([#784](https://github.com/alexwkleung/reika/pull/784)).

## Reviews, reports and testing

- [u/JesseWebDotCom](https://www.reddit.com/user/JesseWebDotCom): a security audit of 0.1.0, posted
  on the launch thread. It led to the project `.env` fix in 0.2.0
  ([#709](https://github.com/alexwkleung/reika/pull/709)) and the sandbox hardening in
  [#706](https://github.com/alexwkleung/reika/issues/706)–[#708](https://github.com/alexwkleung/reika/issues/708).
  They also compared Reika against other coding agents (Aider, OpenCode, Codex CLI) on an M4 Pro 24GB.
- [u/Feisty-Ambassador215](https://www.reddit.com/user/Feisty-Ambassador215): an early report from
  daily-driving Reika on Ornith 1.5 9B.

## Adding your name

Merged pull requests are credited here and in the [changelog](../CHANGELOG.md). Reports and reviews
that change what Reika does count too: [Support](support.md#other-ways-to-help) lists the kinds of
help that matter most, and the [testers issue](https://github.com/alexwkleung/reika/issues/791) is
the place to share a run on your hardware. Contributions of any size are welcome.
